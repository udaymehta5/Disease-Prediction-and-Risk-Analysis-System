Disease: Acute Viral Respiratory Infections (Influenza, COVID-19, Common Cold)
Category: Infectious Respiratory Disease

Overview:
Acute viral respiratory infections are caused by viruses (such as Influenza A/B, SARS-CoV-2, Rhinovirus, Adenovirus, or RSV) affecting the upper and/or lower respiratory tracts, transmitted predominantly through airborne respiratory droplets.

Key Symptoms:
- Sudden onset of high fever (> 38°C / 100.4°F) with shivering or rigors
- Dry, hacking, or productive cough
- Sore throat, pharyngeal pain, and difficulty swallowing
- Severe generalized muscle aches (myalgia) and joint pain
- Profound physical fatigue, malaise, and weakness
- Nasal congestion, rhinorrhea (runny nose), and sneezing
- Loss of taste (ageusia) or smell (anosmia) particularly with SARS-CoV-2

Critical Biomarkers / Lab Indicators:
- Rapid Antigen Diagnostic Test (RADT) or RT-PCR positive for Influenza or SARS-CoV-2
- Complete Blood Count (CBC): Normal or decreased white blood cell count (leukopenia) with relative lymphopenia
- Elevated C-Reactive Protein (CRP) or Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR)
- Pulse Oximetry SpO2 < 94% indicates lower respiratory tract involvement / pneumonia requiring urgent evaluation

Primary Risk Factors:
- Close contact with infected individuals in poorly ventilated or crowded indoor environments
- Lack of up-to-date seasonal influenza or COVID-19 vaccinations
- Compromised immune system (immunosuppressive therapy, HIV, active cancer chemotherapy)
- Extremes of age (< 2 years or > 65 years)
- Chronic comorbid conditions (diabetes, chronic kidney disease, heart failure, asthma)

Precautions & Clinical Management:
- Ensure strict bed rest and drink abundant oral fluids (water, oral rehydration solutions, warm soups)
- Isolate at home to prevent transmitting viral infection to vulnerable family members
- Antipyretic / analgesic medications (acetaminophen or ibuprofen) to manage fever and myalgias
- For high-risk individuals, early prescription antiviral therapy (e.g., Oseltamivir within 48 hours for Flu; Paxlovid for COVID-19)
- Seek immediate emergency care if experiencing cyanosis (blue lips/face), confusion, or severe respiratory distress
